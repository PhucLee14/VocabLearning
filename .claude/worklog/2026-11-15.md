## Session 1: playful interactions, stairs up the mountain, houses as parts of speech

Continues the island work of 2026-11-14 (sessions 4–10 there). The request was made late on the 14th and the session was cut off after the first look at the code; this is the work itself.

### Request
- Apply "group 2" of the interaction ideas (the playful ones): click the sun / moon to change theme, drag to turn the island, small reactions on click, a lantern at night, a mushroom hunt.
- Fewer houses, each standing for a part of speech (noun, verb, adjective, …).
- The mountain's terraces become steps that wind up around it; the mountain may grow.

### Decisions
- Eight houses = the eight classic parts of speech (noun, verb, adjective, adverb, pronoun, preposition, conjunction, interjection). Order in `parts.ts`, kept free of three.js because `island.tsx` (first-load script) needs it for the labels.
- The CEFR levels stay on the mountain, as six flags along the stairs (A1 at the foot, C2 on the summit); the "levels" floor's camera moved to the side of the mountain (angle 200°) where the stairs and flags show.
- The canvas stays `pointer-events-none`. The scene listens on `window` and treats the pointer as "on the island" only when it is not over content (`overPage`). Picking is ray-against-sphere: the island is one merged mesh, so there is nothing finer to hit.
- Dragging works on every floor, not only the hero; a floor change eases the turn back so each floor still starts from its own view. Touch: `touch-pan-y touch-pinch-zoom` on the floors, so vertical swipes still scroll.
- Reduced motion: reactions to a click still play (they are asked for); things that happen by themselves (idle fish jumps, flicker, the sky) stay frozen.
- Labels are decoration (`aria-hidden`). The level facts are in the page's text already; the parts of speech are not: a known gap for keyboard / screen-reader users, noted in PLAN.md.

### Done

- `actors.ts` (new): canoe (lap of the lake on click), fish (jumps where the lake is clicked, and now and then by itself), campfire flame (flares on click), five mushrooms (hop and vanish when found), a point light that is the night lantern (follows the ground under the pointer, rests at the fire otherwise).

- `scene.tsx`: pointer state, drag-to-turn, picking, reactions, hover labels, cursor, tap labels on touch, lab aid `window.__islandTargets()`.

## Session 7: a rounder, lit island (shadows, smooth surfaces, living water, night details)

### Request
- "What can we do to make the models more real and smoother?" I listed five groups (light and shadow; smooth surfaces; living materials; post-processing; more detailed models). The user explained that "smooth" means the shapes should not be so blocky and kinked, and chose groups 1 and 2, then 3.
- Also asked how to see the current fps (answer: the lab page's read-out, or Chrome's FPS meter).

### Decisions
- No more flat shading in the scene: the user's choice overrides the "flat, no shadows" house rule there (PLAN.md and CLAUDE.md say so now). The UI itself is unchanged.
- Smoothness comes from three things, in this order of effect: normals (shared across edges flatter than a crease angle, own per face otherwise), smooth curves through the same layout points, and for tree tops real extra triangles (one level of Phong tessellation). Houses keep their edges.
- Ambient occlusion is baked per vertex by the kit script (it needs ray casting: about 100 million ray-triangle tests, half a minute in Node, far too slow at page load); normals and tessellation are done in the browser when the kit loads (linear time), so the file grew by 13 KB gzip only.
- No tone mapping: three.js does not tone-map the clear colour (our sky), so the fogged cloud sea would stop matching the sky, the problem fixed in session 6. Light levels are set so nothing clips instead.
- Chimney smoke became campfire smoke: the kit's houses do not all have chimneys, and the campfire's place is known.
- Light direction changed: mostly from the sun's side, only a little from the viewer's (`LIGHT_FROM_VIEWER`), so shadows fall across the ground. With the old direction they fell behind the things that cast them.

### Done
- `scripts/island-kit.mjs`: `occlusion()` (48 rays per vertex against the model and the ground under it), file format "GDK2" with one more byte per vertex. Re-baked: 65 models, 17,605 triangles, 219 KB (115 KB gzip).
- `mesh.ts` (new, plain arrays): `weld`, `creasedNormals`, `tessellate`.
- `kit.ts`: reads GDK2; `refine` turns each model into corners with normals (crease angle by role) and tessellates the leaves of trees and bushes.
- `geometry.ts`: helpers for smooth curves (`spline`, `around`, `roundLoop`, `roundLine`, `wave`); the rim, the body's rows, the lake, the patches, the paths and the stream are smooth; the mountain is `rockFace` (smooth surface through the old corners plus two layers of lumps, none in the gully) drawn as a 48 × 32 grid, and `onMountain`, the trail and `cascade` use the same function; colours per vertex instead of per face; `Builder` rewritten (corners, normals, `extra`, single-sided skins that are turned outwards by an inside point); `buildWater` (lake, waterfall, stream, rim fall, with `flow` and `shore`); `Frame`: a theme change only recomputes colours.
- `scene.tsx`: shadow map, `HemisphereLight` instead of the ambient light, new light levels and colours in the theme fade, the island material's patches for wind and night glow, the water mesh, smoke and fireflies.
- `water.ts`: `createWater` (ripples, depth tint, sky at a flat angle, crests, shore foam). `ambience.ts` (new): smoke and fireflies as soft points. `scenery.ts`: round clouds, `cloudMaterial` (lit per corner), two levels of detail for the cloud sea. `actors.ts`: canoe, fish and mushrooms cast shadows.

### Problems
- "No shadows" for about an hour. They were there all along: (1) the light came half from the viewer's side, so shadows fell behind things; (2) in full frames scaled down for viewing, soft shadows on pale grass cannot be seen. Found by reading pixels with `gl.readPixels` and by enlarged crops. Also needed: `shadow.camera.updateProjectionMatrix()` after changing the shadow camera's box.
- Headless Chrome can use the real GPU on this machine (`--enable-gpu --ignore-gpu-blocklist --use-angle=d3d11`): real frame rates and correct rendering. All test scripts now use it.
- Smoke as solid spheres looked like a snowman: now soft transparent points.
- The first build of the island took about 100 ms, and so did every theme change: `Frame` brings a theme change down to about 14 ms.
- Tried and removed: drawing the cloud sea nearest-first (no measurable gain).
- Long bash heredocs with apostrophes failed again: patch scripts are written with the Write tool.

### Verification
- Lint, tsc and `next build` clean. Scene chunk 157 KB gzip (was 150).
- On this machine's integrated Radeon, headless, lab page: about 130–144 fps at 1600×900 ×1; 60–75 fps at 1920×1080 ×1.5 (about 75–80 with shadows off; about 127 at ×1). Kit refine 40–135 ms once at load, 374 ms with the CPU throttled 4×.
- Earlier scripts pass on the GPU: interactions, mushroom hunt (5 of 5), double-click zoom, kit blocked (plain shapes, with shadows and water), page fade. Screenshots: day, night, mid-fade, phone layout, reduced motion. No console output.
- Not verified: the wind and the ripples in motion (stills only), real phones, Safari, a discrete or older GPU.
