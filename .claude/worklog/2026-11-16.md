## Session 1: water with body and depth, people, a quality guard

### Request
After seeing the rounder, lit island (session 7 of 2026-11-15) the user said: the resolution guard I had offered sounds good; the trees are fine; the lake and the running water are too simple, the running water is too thin, has no visible movement and no depth; and, if possible, add some people (fishing, walking, on the mountain, reading, …).

### What happened around it
- Between my last action and the user's "continue", the user committed the whole working tree to `sky-island` (`3268eb8`, pushed; it holds everything up to and including the first version of `people.ts`) and switched to `main` for an unrelated commit. The island does not exist on `main`, so the lab page was a 404 and it first looked as if the work was gone. Nothing was lost. ### Decisions
- Depth in the lake is drawn, not modelled: the ground is one flat disc, and digging a real basin for the lake and a trench for the stream would mean re-triangulating the ground around a hole that reaches the rim. The material shows a bed under the surface instead, shifted with the angle of view (parallax) and covered by the water's colour the deeper it is.
- Body for running water is modelled: five strips across, arched; the waterfall up to 1.15 wide (0.75 before), the stream 1 (0.7).
- The foam streaks (quads sliding down the falls) are gone: in screenshots they read as dashes on a road. Movement now comes from two layers of streaks in the material and from spray.
- People are built from stretched spheres, in code. A kit of characters would have needed a new bake pipeline (parts, pivots, new colour roles) for figures that are 15–40 pixels tall most of the time. Said so to the user.
- The guard only goes down, never up, so it cannot flip to and fro; a reload resets it.

### Done
- `geometry.ts`: `cascade` with a swell and a narrow spring; `RIM_FALL` arc; `SPLASHES`; `buildWater` with a generic `run` (five lines across) and `flow` as four numbers; `WATER_STREAMS` removed; for people: `WALKS`, `BRIDGES`, `BRIDGE_DECK`, `trailWalk`, `lakeside`, `PEOPLE_SPOTS`.
- `water.ts`: rewritten (bed, caustics, depth, ripples, lapping foam; broad and fine streaks, edge foam, swell, break-up of the rim fall). The streak mesh is gone.
- `ambience.ts`: `createPuffs` (shared by smoke and spray), `createSpray`.
- `people.ts` (new): eight people, poses, walking along routes, the hiker on the trail, greeting on click, colours that ease with the theme.
- `scene.tsx`: wiring of the above; `Kind` `'person'`; the quality guard (`DPR_CAPS`, `lowerQuality`).
- `actors.ts`: `canoeHeading`.

### Problems

- The reader first sat on the edge of the mountain trail (the tree stands right beside it): moved to the tree's other side.

## Session 2: Ctrl + wheel zooms the island

### Request
- The user asked what I thought of zooming with the wheel while hovering over the island. I advised against the plain wheel (it moves from floor to floor, and the island fills half the screen, so it would swallow most attempts to scroll) and offered three ways; the user chose the first: Ctrl + wheel, which also covers a trackpad pinch.

### Done
- `scene.tsx`: a `wheel` listener (not passive). With Ctrl or Cmd held and the pointer over the bare island it cancels the event (so the browser does not zoom the page) and adds the wheel up; every `WHEEL_PER_ZOOM_STEP` (60; a mouse notch is about 100) is one step of `ZOOM_STEPS`. Zooming in first moves the zoom's focus to what the pointer is on. A pause of 0.4 s starts a new gesture. `floor-scroll.ts` already ignored Ctrl + wheel.
- `island.tsx`: a one-time hint pill ("Hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom") when a mouse first rests on the island; it goes after 7 s, or for good once the visitor has zoomed. New message `Island.zoomHint` in both languages.

### Verification
- Scripted on `/vi` at 1600×900: the hint appears on the first hover; Ctrl + notch up → step 1, three more → the last step (zoom-in button disabled); Ctrl + notch down → one step back; twelve small pieces (a pinch) → back to the start, and the hint does not return; Ctrl + wheel over the text panel does nothing to the island; the plain wheel still goes to the next floor and resets the zoom; `scrollY` stays 0 throughout the zooming. Interactions and double-click scripts still pass. Lint, tsc and `next build` clean.
- Not verified: a real trackpad pinch (simulated as small Ctrl + wheel pieces), Safari, and macOS where Ctrl + scroll may be taken by the system's own zoom if that accessibility option is on.

## Session 3: bridges the right way round, walkers on the deck, slower walking

### Request
- With a screenshot: a bridge seems to point the wrong way, walkers go through the bridge, and the walkers should be a little slower.

### Found
- Both bridges lay along the water instead of across it. The note "the kit's bridge runs along its z axis" (work log 2026-11-14, CLAUDE.md) was wrong: listing the model's long triangles shows the rails on its ±z sides running along x, the deck at y = 0.15 and sloping ends beyond x = ±0.4. So it is walked along x.
- The walkers' height was inverted. `MathUtils.smoothstep(x, min, max)` returns 0 for every x below `min`, and I had passed the larger bound first (0.95, 0.5): walkers were 0.17 above the ground everywhere and dropped to the ground on a bridge. Nobody had noticed the floating; on the bridge it showed as walking through the deck.

### Done
- `geometry.ts`: `BRIDGE_SPOTS` now holds place, turn and scale; the turn comes from the path's own direction at the bridge (`bearing(...) - 90`), so the deck carries the path. Scales 1.65 and 1.7 (were 1.55 and 1.4) so the sloping ends stand clear of the water, which is wider since this morning. `BRIDGE_DECK` 0.25, `BRIDGE_FLAT`, `BRIDGE_END`.
- `people.ts`: `footing` fixed (on the deck within `BRIDGE_FLAT`, down to the ground at `BRIDGE_END`); `WALK_SPEED` 0.42 → 0.28; the hiker's round trip 190 → 300 s.
- CLAUDE.md: the kit fact about the bridge corrected.

### Verification
- Crops on the GPU: both bridges cross their water with the rails along the path; a villager caught on the stream's bridge stands on the deck between the rails. tsc, lint and `next build` clean; the interaction script passes.
- Not verified: a walker on the second bridge (none crossed it in the two minutes watched; same code path), the new pace in motion.
