# Work log 2026-11-17

## Session 1: sharp floating words; floors that always alternate

### Requests
- "The floating words are still blurry."
- Then: "the page's floors are not staggered yet" (text and island not alternating sides).

### Found
- Blurry words: they were canvas textures on sprites. The canvas was drawn 3.6 times larger than shown and scaled down, and the scene itself is drawn at a pixel ratio of at most 1.5 (0.8 when the quality guard steps in), lower than a 2× screen's: the text was resampled twice.
- Floors: the landing page had only floors 0, 2, 3 and 5. `try` and `topics` are only rendered when `landingData()` returns words and topics; it had been called while the backend was down, and the empty answer was cached with `cacheLife('hours')`. The sides alternate by stop number, so 0 and 2 were both on the left and 3 and 5 both on the right.

### Done
- `words.ts` rewritten as HTML: a pool of pill elements (same look as the pointer's label, page font and tokens) on a layer appended to the canvas's container, so it sits over the island and under the page's panels and is `aria-hidden` with it. Each frame the word's point is projected with the camera and the element placed with a transform snapped to whole device pixels. `scene.tsx`: `createWords(layer, toWorld)`, one set in the island's space and one in the camera's for the sun and moon; the colour plumbing for the old canvases removed (the tokens do it now, theme fade included).
- `stop.tsx`: `xl:[&:nth-of-type(even)]:justify-end` (order on the page) instead of the stop number. `island.tsx`: `readSides` works out the island's side per stop from the same order (a missing floor's stop takes the next floor's side) into a `sides` ref; `scene.tsx` reads it instead of `stop % 2`.
- `landing-data.ts`: `cacheLife('hours')` on success, `cacheLife('minutes')` on failure (the conditional pattern from the Next 16 `cacheLife` docs).
- CLAUDE.md updated.

### Verification
- Words on a 2× screen: as sharp as the page's own text.
- All six floors back after the change (the cache entry changed with the code); text left, right, left, … on all six at 1440×810. With floors 1 and 4 removed from the page by script: 0 left, 2 right, 3 left, 5 right, and the island opposite the text on each (screenshots).
- tsc, lint, `next build` clean (`/vi` static, revalidate 1 h). Interaction, mushroom hunt, double-click and Ctrl + wheel scripts pass.
- Not verified: the production page while the API is down (only simulated by removing the sections), touch devices, Safari.
