### What happened around it

### Decisions
- Still figures made in code (no character kit): with knees, elbows, hands, shoes, eyes, hair and hats they read as people; a kit would need a new bake pipeline for figures that are small on screen.
- Bigger: 0.68 world units instead of 0.5 (about the true scale next to the houses). At true scale nothing they did could be made out even fully zoomed in.
- Every person gets a loop with a beginning and an end, and a prop that says what they do, instead of a pose that wobbles.
- Arms that hold something are solved from where the hand must be (two-bone IK), so hands stay on the rod, hoe, paddle, book and stick.

### Done
- `people.ts` rewritten. Per person 18 spheres (thighs, shins, shoes, torso, upper arms, forearms, hands, head, hair or hat crown, eyes, hat or brim); round props (rod, line, float, fish, walking stick, hoe handle, paddle shaft, toasting stick and its treat) in the same instanced mesh; a second instanced mesh of boxes (book covers and the page being turned, hoe blade, paddle blade, backpack, basket, bucket).
  - Fisher: waits (float bobbing, a nibble), bite (float under, rod tip tugged), strike, reels in (right hand circling), holds the fish up, lets it drop back, rod back over the shoulder, flick, the float flies out. 16 s.
  - Villagers: knees bend as a leg swings through, arms swing, the body twists a little; they slow at the ends and look round; one carries a basket.
  - Hiker: backpack, cap, walking stick planted ahead in step; bent into the climb, upright going down.
  - Reader: back to a tree, knees up, a red book open on them, head bowed; a page turns over every 7 s (the right hand follows it); now and then looks up.
  - Camper: on the ground between the stump and the fire (it used to sit on the stump, which is taller than a person's legs), toasting on a stick just short of the flames; pulls it back to blow on it.
  - Farmer: hoe raised slowly over the head, brought down fast, a short pull; every fourth swing straightens up and looks round.
  - Rower: on the canoe's seat (higher than before, so the body shows over the side), paddling with a bladed paddle out over the side, three strokes a side; strokes faster while the canoe does a lap.
- `actors.ts`: the canoe drifts round the lake between laps (`CANOE_DRIFT`), so the paddling always moves it; `canoePace()`.
- `geometry.ts`: the camper's spot moved off the stump.
- CLAUDE.md updated.
