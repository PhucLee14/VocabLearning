## Session 3: a deep blue sky, soft clouds, a smooth sea of clouds

### Request
- With a photo (a sea of clouds under a vivid blue sky): make the clouds and the sea of clouds smoother, and the daytime sky that fresh blue.

### Done
- `scenery.ts`:
  - `createSkyDome`: a sphere around the camera (renderOrder -1, no depth), horizon colour (the fog's) to the colour overhead within about 5° (`smoothstep(-0.005, 0.08, up)`), since the camera never looks much higher.
  - `createCloudSea`: one surface (96 rings × 160, closer together near the island, radius 320) raised by noise into rounded billows (7 high, 30 wide) in the vertex shader, normal from two neighbouring samples, wrap lighting so there is no hard edge to the light, fine wisps and greyer hollows in the fragment shader; billows and wisps drift with `time`. Replaces 460 flattened spheres and a disc.
  - `createClouds`: seven camera-facing soft puffs per cloud (one instanced quad mesh): a round blot with a ragged edge from noise, lit top, slightly greyer flat underside; transparent, drawn after the island. Replaces clouds of merged spheres.
  - Shared noise GLSL (`NOISE`); the old `cloudMaterial` and `cloudGeometry` removed.
- Tokens: `--sky` (light) `#d3e9f8` → `#c2def5`; new `--sky-top` `#1f72dc` (light) and `#03070f` (dark); `palette.ts` reads it as `skyTop`; the scene fades the dome's overhead colour with the theme like the other colours.
- `celestial.ts`: the stars are blended over the sky (custom blending, like the sun and moon) instead of mixing with one sky colour, since the sky is no longer one colour.

### Problems
- The far edge of the cloud sea stood out as a jagged line against the sky. Cause: in the cloud shaders `fog_fragment` came before `colorspace_fragment`, but three hands the fog's colour over already in the output colour space (its own materials apply fog after the colour-space step), so the fog came out lighter than the sky. Found by reading the material's `fogColor` uniform and pixels above and below the edge; fixed by swapping the two includes. The old cloud material had the same bug, which is why a horizon line showed before too.
- The blue first only reached the very top of the screen: the gradient was spread over 25°, but the camera sees at most about 5° above the horizon. Tightened.

### Verification
- GPU screenshots by day (floors 0, 1, 3, landing page) and night, and in the middle of a theme change: the sea of clouds melts into the horizon with no edge; on low floors the sky is a clear deep blue above a pale band; clouds are white, soft heaps; night still dark with stars and moon.
- tsc, lint, `next build` clean. Interaction and mushroom-hunt scripts pass.
- Frame rate went up: 1920×1080 ×1.5 about 65–77 fps without the quality guard stepping down (before: 46–66, and it stepped down to 1.25); 1600×900 up to the 144 Hz cap.
- Not verified: motion at full speed (the drift of billows and wisps), Safari, phones.
