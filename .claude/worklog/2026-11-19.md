## Session 2: the wheel "jumps back" to the floor it came from

### Request
"Why does scrolling sometimes snap back to the old position?" The user also pasted a Turbopack parse error in `island.tsx`.

### The parse error
- It was transient. Turbopack compiled `island.tsx` between two of my edits, while the new `<>` was not yet closed.
- The finished file compiled. The user committed it as 2fa241c, "Go up" button.

### Cause, reproduced with puppeteer-core
Method: real wheel events through CDP, with `scrollY` logged every frame.

**Case 1: a floor slightly taller than the screen.**
- At 1440×700 the "how" floor is 717px tall. `useFloorWheel` treated it as a tall floor and left the notch to native scrolling (`distance > innerHeight + 8`).
- The native scroll went past the floor's end, and mandatory snap pulled the page back to that end (1418).
- 1418 was not in the list of rest positions, which held floor tops only, so the next notch was native again.
- Result: stuck until a hard flick.

**Case 2: below 1280px.**
- `useFloorWheel` was off there, and proximity snap pulled every notch back to the end of a tall floor. Stuck at 1200×800.
- The hero floor is taller than the screen at 900×700 and 390×700, and its end was not a snap point, so the page went back to 0.

### Fix
`floor-scroll.ts`:
- `restingPlaces()` returns stretches (`{from, to, free}`) instead of floor tops:
  - a floor whose content shows whole at its top is one stretch, `[top, end]`;
  - a floor whose content would be cut off gets two places, top and end;
  - when the end is more than a screen past the top, the gap between them is `free` and scrolled natively.
- The wheel always targets the next place's `from`.
- The hook now runs at every width. The `WIDE` constant is gone.

`stop.tsx`:
- The first floor has `snap-end`, so its end is a snap point.
- While the hero fits on the screen, that point is the same place as the header's.

`CLAUDE.md`: the breakpoint now lives in three places; the floor-scroll notes are updated.

### Verification
Walked every floor by single notches, down to the footer and back up. No stuck step and no jump back at any of these sizes:
- 1440×900
- 1440×700
- 1280×600
- 1200×800
- 1024×640
- 900×700
- 390×700

Before the fix, the first two failing sizes:
- 1440×700 stuck at 1418;
- 1200×800 jumped back at 1774.

Trackpad flicks with momentum move exactly one floor.

`npm run typecheck` and `eslint src/components/island` pass.

- Cancelling it would also cancel Chrome/Safari's two-finger swipe back to the previous page.
