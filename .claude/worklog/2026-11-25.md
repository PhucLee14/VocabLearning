# 2026-11-25

## Session 1: landing buttons no longer cover the floor panels on phones

### Request
Fix the open item from 2026-11-19: on phones, the bottom-right controls covered a corner of the floor panel's text. The controls are "back to top", zoom in and zoom out.

### Decisions
- **Below 640px (`sm`)**:
  - The controls are a **row** at the bottom right, not a column.
  - The floors' bottom padding is `pb-16` instead of `pb-8` (`stop.tsx`), so a panel resting on a floor ends above the row.
  - The mushroom counter moves to the bottom left, with `max-w-[calc(100vw-12rem)]`, beside the row instead of under it.
- **From 640px** nothing changes: a column at the bottom right, the counter centred.
  - Below 1280px the panel is at most 520px wide, so it can't reach the right edge there.
  - From 1280px it sits beside the island.
- Rejected:
  - Moving the controls to the top right on phones: a tall floor's panel reaches the top of the screen when scrolled to its end.
  - Hiding the zoom buttons on phones.

### Verification
Method:
- I ran my own `next dev` and `nest start --watch` on 3300 and 4300, then stopped all four PIDs.
- With puppeteer-core, at every resting place of the landing page, I compared the controls' box with every line of text in the floors' panels.

Sizes checked:
- 360×640, 375×667, 390×844, 412×915, 639×800: no line of text is under the controls wherever the panel ends on screen.
  - Text is covered only at the **top** of a floor taller than the screen. There the panel runs off the bottom edge anyway, and that text shows in full at the floor's end.
- 640×800, 768×1024, 1024×700, 1280×720, 1440×900: no overlap at all.

Other checks:
- Screenshot at 390×844 (the demo-card floor): the row sits under the panel.
- `npm run typecheck` and `eslint src/components/island` pass.
- The mushroom counter's new place was not seen live: it only appears after a mushroom is found by clicking.
